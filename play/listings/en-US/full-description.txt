Retro-3DO is a new emulator implementation for the 3DO Interactive Multiplayer, built for Android phones, tablets and gaming handhelds.

Features:
• Vulkan and OpenGL ES rendering
• Custom Adreno/Turnip driver support on compatible devices
• ISO, BIN/CUE, IMG and CHD game images
• Multi-disc libraries with live disc swapping
• Physical gamepads and configurable on-screen controls
• 4:3 and 16:9 display modes, optional bezels and CRT effects
• 50/60 Hz system modes and optional CPU boost while preserving audio and video timing
• A–Z library filters, cover artwork integration and one-touch rescanning

Retro-3DO does not include a BIOS or games. You must supply legally obtained 3DO BIOS and game images. Compatibility is under active development.
